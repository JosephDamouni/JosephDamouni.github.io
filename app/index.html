Desk Gadget

Connect your Google account once. Paste the sign-in file from your laptop's Desk Gadget:

  1. On the laptop, press Windows + R, type %APPDATA%\DeskGadget and open token.json in Notepad.
  2. Copy everything in it and get it to this phone (for example, email it to yourself, then delete that email).
  3. Paste it below and tap Connect.

It stays on this phone only. Nothing is sent anywhere except to Google.
