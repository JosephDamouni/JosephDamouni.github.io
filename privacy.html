Privacy Policy

Desk Gadget · Last updated 24 October 2026

Desk Gadget is a personal tool used only by its owner, Joseph (Rani) Damouni, with his own Google account. It has no other users and is not distributed to anyone.

What it accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): message headers and text, to show important messages and to spot course emails about homework.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): upcoming events, to show them in the panel.
  • Google Tasks (read and write): to show open tasks, add tasks, and mark tasks as done.

How the data is used

Data is used only to display it to the owner on his own laptop and phone, and to create tasks in his own Google Tasks. Desk Gadget does not send, sell, share or transfer any Google user data to anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train any AI or machine-learning model.

Where data is stored

Nothing is stored on any server. The app talks directly to Google's APIs from the owner's own devices. On those devices it keeps only the Google sign-in token and a few small settings files (for example, which emails were hidden from the panel). Email content is not saved.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Desk Gadget's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's folder and its sign-in file removes everything it stored.
